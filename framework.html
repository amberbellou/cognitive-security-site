The Research Framework

The collection, operationalized for study design: for every threat subclass — the mechanisms in evidence, the paradigms that measure it, and the protections with evidence behind them. Every line is generated from a verified extraction record; every citation links to the Threat Map card carrying the mechanically proven quotes. The page rebuilds with the collection.

Designing a study with this page: 1 · pick the threat subclass your question lives in   2 · adopt a measurement paradigm from “How to measure it” (cited, replicable)   3 · state the mechanism you expect   4 · test a candidate protection against it   5 · let the evidence-tier chips on the Threat Map calibrate how much weight each source can bear (see the curation policy on the About page).

T1Attacks on Belief Formation

Threats that corrupt what people come to believe — not by breaking the machine, but by exploiting how human belief updating works.

T1aSycophantic reinforcement19 papers

AI agreement loops inflate confidence without adding truth.

Mechanisms in evidence

  • Scale and preference-model reward shape agreement-seeking and self-preservation expressions — Perez E 2022
  • Preference data rewards belief-matching: humans and preference models often pick well-written agreement over correctness — Sharma M 2024
  • Survey: causes in preference optimization; links to hallucination and bias — Malmqvist L 2024
  • Belief updating: hypothesis-conditioned feedback amplifies confirmation bias — Batista R M 2026
  • Face preservation beyond propositional agreement — affirming self-image and implicit beliefs — Cheng M 2025
  • Rebuttal pressure flips model answers; preemptive rebuttals strongest — Fanous A 2025
  • Two-stage internal emergence: late-layer output preference shift, then representational divergence — Li J 2025
  • Opinion formation: models mirror the user under argumentative pressure, masking persona-independent positions — Nogueira R 2026
  • Conversational conformity: models abandon correct answers/abstentions when users introduce suggestions — Guo K 2026
  • Conformity: models abandon correct answers under simulated peer pressure — Qu J 2026
  • Trust formation: users detect and penalize belief-aligned falsehood — Carro M V 2024
  • Model internals: sycophantic agreement and praise are distinct linear directions in activation space — Vennemeyer D 2026
  • Iterative feedback, challenges, and persuasion erode initially correct positions — Liu J 2025
  • Rebuttal pressure distorts evaluation independent of argument quality — Kim S 2025
  • Turn-of-Flip and Number-of-Flip quantify conformity dynamics across turns — Hong J 2026
  • Steering and consistency functions that entrench reliance — Jacobowitz S 2026
  • Benchmark accuracy masks epistemic fragility in interactive clinical use — Xiao B 2026
  • Fine-tuning on data where claim truth is decoupled from user opinion reduces agreement-seeking — Wei J 2024
  • Situational awareness + sycophancy toward the perceived evaluator — Baek D D 2026

How to measure it — paradigms & instruments

  • 154 model-written evaluation sets applied across model sizes and RLHF stages (Anthropic) — Perez E 2022
  • Behavioral evaluation of five assistants + analysis of human preference data (Anthropic, ICLR 2024) — Sharma M 2024
  • Bayesian rational analysis + Wason 2-4-6 rule-discovery experiment, N=557 — Batista R M 2026
  • ELEPHANT benchmark applied to 11 models vs human baselines — Cheng M 2025
  • Framework across ChatGPT-4o, Claude-Sonnet, Gemini on math and medical datasets (Stanford) — Fanous A 2025
  • Logit-lens analysis and causal activation patching across model families (KAUST/PKU) — Li J 2025
  • llm-bias-bench: direct + indirect multi-turn probing, 3 personas, 38 topics, 13 assistants (Brazilian Portuguese) — Nogueira R 2026
  • Stick-or-switch framework; 17 LLMs across 3 clinical benchmarks — Guo K 2026
  • Controlled multi-agent study: 4 open-weight LLMs x 7 QA datasets; manipulated consensus structure and authority labels — Qu J 2026
  • Between-subjects experiment: sycophantic GPT vs standard ChatGPT; behavioral + self-report trust measures — Carro M V 2024
  • Interpretability: difference-in-means directions, activation additions, subspace geometry across model families — Vennemeyer D 2026
  • Multi-turn benchmark across model families (arXiv) — Liu J 2025
  • Controlled rebuttal experiments on evaluator LLMs (Johns Hopkins, arXiv) — Kim S 2025
  • SYCON-Bench: 17 LLMs, six families, three scenarios (CMU/Emory, EMNLP Findings) — Hong J 2026
  • Epistemic-resilience benchmark under clinical pressure scenarios (HIT, arXiv) — Xiao B 2026
  • Synthetic-data intervention evaluated across model scales (Google DeepMind) — Wei J 2024
  • Behavioral experiments manipulating perceived researcher/evaluation context (MIT/GWU) — Baek D D 2026

Protections with evidence

  • Model-written evaluations. Automated generation of behavioral evals enables rapid discovery and tracking of emerging behaviors like sycophancy. — Perez E 2022
  • Mitigation taxonomy. Training-data improvements, fine-tuning methods, post-deployment controls, and decoding strategies, with trade-offs. — Malmqvist L 2024
  • Unbiased information sampling. Design AI feedback to sample from the true distribution rather than conditioning on the user's hypothesis; quintupled discovery rates in the experiment. — Batista R M 2026
  • Social-sycophancy benchmarking. Extends measurement to implicit sycophancy so mitigation isn't gamed by avoiding explicit agreement. — Cheng M 2025
  • Progressive/regressive measurement frame. Distinguishing beneficial from harmful flips gives protection research a precise outcome variable. — Fanous A 2025
  • Mechanistic targets. Identifying the two-stage internal pathway gives intervention points for model-side suppression. — Li J 2025
  • Auditable transparency probes. Open-source, runnable probe (llm-bias-bench) that any team can apply to any assistant/topic/locale to surface hidden opinion bias before deployment. — Nogueira R 2026
  • Targeted activation steering. Suppress sycophantic agreement or praise independently without damaging genuine agreement — a model-side mitigation lever. — Vennemeyer D 2026
  • Flip-based measurement. Turn-of-Flip/Number-of-Flip give protection research precise conformity metrics; reasoning optimization strengthens resistance. — Hong J 2026
  • Resilience benchmarking. Pre-deployment stress-testing of belief stability for clinical LLMs. — Xiao B 2026
  • Synthetic decoupling data. A lightweight fine-tuning step teaching truth-independence from user opinion measurably reduces sycophancy. — Wei J 2024
  • Evaluation-context controls. Lab studies of sycophancy must mask evaluator identity or model results confound with performative alignment. — Baek D D 2026

T1bPersuasion & manipulation12 papers

Personalized, adaptive, truth-agnostic influence at scale.

Mechanisms in evidence

  • Personalization: basic sociodemographic data amplifies conversational persuasive power — Salvi F 2024
  • Multi-turn conversation lets models deploy information and personalization strategically — Hackenburg K 2025
  • User data integrated into prompts generates individually tailored persuasion — Hackenburg K 2024
  • Persuasive capability vs model scale: log-linear growth that flattens — Hackenburg K 2025
  • Message-to-personality matching automated by generative models — Matz S C 2024
  • Fine-tuning to individuals creates per-user persuasion and echo-chamber surfaces — Kirk H R 2023
  • Paltering: selective emphasis and decontextualized truths imply false conclusions without fabrication — Costello T H 2026
  • Belief change rides on evidence quality, not perceived source identity — Boissin E 2025
  • Closed-loop persuasion: agents read reactions and adapt tactics against individual users in real time — Rosenberg L 2023
  • Generated content influencing beliefs and behaviour at scale; harmful vs legitimate influence remains hard to delimit — Bengio Y (Chair) et al. 2026
  • Pedagogical gap: diagnosis of disempowerment exists but concrete interventions are underexplored — Komissarov A 2026
  • Fluency decoupled from understanding; trust display without stakes; offloading of evaluation; optimization-produced sycophancy — Maynard A D 2026

How to measure it — paradigms & instruments

  • Preregistered RCT, 2x2 factorial (human/LLM x personalization), N=820, live multi-round debates — Salvi F 2024
  • Large-scale experiments across models and prompting strategies (Science) — Hackenburg K 2025
  • Preregistered RCT with a live GPT-4 microtargeting web application (PNAS) — Hackenburg K 2024
  • 720 persuasive messages from 24 models across sizes, tested experimentally (PNAS) — Hackenburg K 2025
  • Multi-study experiments with ChatGPT-generated tailored messages (Scientific Reports) — Matz S C 2024
  • Three preregistered experiments, N=2,724 Americans, GPT-4o instructed to bunk vs debunk — Costello T H 2026
  • Preregistered experiment, N=955, source framing manipulated (PNAS Nexus) — Boissin E 2025

Protections with evidence

  • Personal-data firewalls. The persuasion advantage rides on personalization — limiting AI access to user attributes is a governance lever. — Salvi F 2024
  • Bounded personalisation. Policy framework drawing explicit limits on what may be personalised and by whom. — Kirk H R 2023
  • Corrective dialogue + accuracy prompting. A corrective conversation reversed induced beliefs; prompting for accuracy-only information sharply reduced bunking power. — Costello T H 2026
  • Evidence-first correction. Corrective power generalizes across messengers — invest in evidence quality, not messenger identity. — Boissin E 2025
  • Ban closing the loop. Bar platforms from tracking real-time emotional reactions (voice, face, biometrics) for persuasive purposes. — Rosenberg L 2023
  • International risk taxonomy and evaluation consensus. Shared categories, safety evaluation practice, and governance priorities across countries. — Bengio Y (Chair) et al. 2026
  • Inoculation-based AI literacy curriculum. Eight Learning Outcomes teaching cognitive resistance to AI disempowerment before exposure. — Komissarov A 2026
  • Vigilance-aware design. Naming the four bypass routes gives protection design concrete targets. — Maynard A D 2026

T1cMisinformation & belief distortion6 papers

Synthetic falsehood outcompetes truth; corrections underperform.

Mechanisms in evidence

  • Credibility asymmetry: clearer, better-structured synthetic text carries false content more persuasively — Spitale G 2023
  • Belief formation: people sample little data, then resist revision once certain — Kidd C 2023
  • Continued influence: corrected misinformation keeps shaping reasoning; familiarity and worldview drive truth judgments — Ecker U K H 2022
  • Inoculation: pre-exposure to weakened manipulation confers psychological resistance — Traberg C S 2022
  • Technique-level inoculation: teaching manipulation tactics confers resistance independent of topic — Roozenbeek J 2022
  • Systematization: nine intervention types spanning nudges, boosts, inoculation, debunking, and friction — Kozyreva A 2024

How to measure it — paradigms & instruments

  • Preregistered study, N=697, judging accuracy and origin of organic vs GPT-3 tweets — Spitale G 2023
  • Lab experiments + large-scale YouTube field experiment with five prebunking videos (Science Advances) — Roozenbeek J 2022

Protections with evidence

  • Accuracy-regulated training data + source disclosure. Train on verified, transparent datasets and have models report generation sources for independent fact-checking. — Spitale G 2023
  • Audits including user calibration. Audit not just model fabrication/bias but how knowledgeable users think systems are and how much they trust outputs. — Kidd C 2023
  • Evidence-based debunking protocol. Warn before repeating the myth, repeat it only once, emphasize the correction, deliver via credible and socially connected sources. — Ecker U K H 2022
  • Prebunking. Technique-based inoculation (games, videos) generalizes across content and outperforms post-hoc correction. — Traberg C S 2022
  • Scalable prebunking videos. Short technique-based videos deployed as ads improved manipulation recognition in the field — the strongest scalability evidence for any cognitive defense in this collection. — Roozenbeek J 2022
  • Intervention toolbox. Nine categorized intervention types with empirical evidence per type — the direct template for structuring an AI cognitive-security protection framework. — Kozyreva A 2024

T1dMachine deception & invisible failure3 papers

Systems that mislead — or fail without any signal the user can see.

Mechanisms in evidence

  • Systematic inducement of false beliefs in pursuit of outcomes other than truth — Park P S 2023
  • Human perceptual detection vs model detection have complementary error patterns — Groh M 2021
  • Failure opacity: users cannot recalibrate trust on failures they never detect — Potts C 2026

How to measure it — paradigms & instruments

  • Large-scale experiments comparing crowds, a leading model, and machine-advised crowds (PNAS) — Groh M 2021
  • Annotation of 100K WildChat human-AI interactions + counterfactual replication with three frontier models — Potts C 2026

Protections with evidence

  • Risk-assessment regulation + bot-or-not laws + detection research. Subject deception-capable systems to robust risk assessment; mandate AI self-identification; fund deception-detection tools. — Park P S 2023
  • Machine-informed crowds. Crowds advised by models outperform either alone — but wrong model advice sways people, so advice quality is critical. — Groh M 2021
  • Invisible-failure monitoring taxonomy. Eight computable failure archetypes support continuous monitoring and map to targeted interventions (calibration, alignment verification, interaction design). — Potts C 2026

How to measure it — paradigms & instruments

T1fMoral judgment manipulation3 papers

AI advice sways ethical decisions while users underestimate it.

Mechanisms in evidence

  • Four social roles — role model, advisor, partner, delegate — through which machines influence ethics; enablers let people offload blame — Köbis N 2021
  • Advice-taking without source discounting: bot disclosure does not immunize — Krügel S 2023
  • Two intervention families: nudges exploit cognitive shortcuts to steer; boosts build lasting competences — Hertwig R 2017

How to measure it — paradigms & instruments

  • Experiment with trolley-dilemma advice from ChatGPT (Scientific Reports) — Krügel S 2023

Protections with evidence

  • Behavioral oversight agenda. Calls for behavioral experiments and oversight designed around the four influence roles. — Köbis N 2021
  • Digital literacy training. Authors propose user training plus better bot design; transparency alone is insufficient. — Krügel S 2023
  • Boost paradigm. Competence-building interventions (rather than choice steering) preserve autonomy — the philosophical foundation for cognitive-security protections that empower users. — Hertwig R 2017

T2Erosion of Cognitive Capability

Threats that degrade the capacity to think — skills, knowledge, and effort atrophying under delegation.

T2aCognitive offloading & critical-thinking decline12 papers

Delegated thinking atrophies; trust displaces verification.

Mechanisms in evidence

  • Physical action substitutes for internal processing, governed by metacognitive evaluations — Risko E F 2016
  • Source confusion between accessible and possessed understanding — Fisher M 2015
  • Cognitive offloading: delegating thinking to AI conserves effort but atrophies critical evaluation — Gerlich M 2025
  • Confidence calibration: trust in AI substitutes for one's own verification effort — Lee H-P 2025
  • Cognitive fatigue partially mediates the dependence-to-critical-thinking pathway — Tian J 2025
  • Functional interference, neurochemical dysregulation, structural neuroplasticity, psychological/metacognitive substitution — Žnidarič U 2026
  • Moral skills, like any skills, require practice that delegation removes — Vallor S 2014
  • Prompting, evaluating outputs, and workflow delegation all demand metacognitive monitoring users often lack — Tankelevitch L 2024
  • AI's appeal (productivity, objectivity) masks epistemic risks that standard practices don't catch — Messeri L 2024
  • Restructures Bloom's levels for cognition distributed between human and AI — Ayodele K P 2026
  • Stable individual difference: the tendency to engage in and enjoy effortful thinking — Cacioppo J T 1982
  • Field-building: connects the science of GenAI's cognitive effects with protective design practice — Tankelevitch L 2025

How to measure it — paradigms & instruments

  • Nine experiments with search vs no-search induction (JEP: General) — Fisher M 2015
  • Mixed methods: surveys + interviews, N=666 across age/education groups; ANOVA, correlation, random forest — Gerlich M 2025
  • Survey of 319 knowledge workers; 936 first-hand GenAI task examples; quantitative + qualitative analysis — Lee H-P 2025
  • Moderated mediation model (PROCESS Model 8), N=580 Chinese university students — Tian J 2025
  • Four scale-development and validation studies (JPSP) — Cacioppo J T 1982

Protections with evidence

  • Metacognitive framework. Offloading decisions follow metacognitive beliefs — miscalibrated confidence, not tools per se, drives maladaptive offloading. — Risko E F 2016
  • Educational strategies for critical engagement. Foster active verification and critical engagement with AI outputs; education level buffers the effect. — Gerlich M 2025
  • Design for critical engagement. Tool design can preserve verification and stewardship roles rather than encouraging wholesale delegation. — Lee H-P 2025
  • Digital/AI literacy programs. Literacy moderates the harm pathway — but programs must manage the added cognitive load they create. — Tian J 2025
  • Moral upskilling design. Technology can also scaffold moral practice — the deskilling/upskilling fork is a design choice. — Vallor S 2014
  • Metacognitive support in design. Integrate support strategies and reduce demand via explainability and customizability. — Tankelevitch L 2024
  • Epistemic risk awareness in research design. Names the illusion types (explanatory depth, exploratory breadth, objectivity) so research communities can guard against them. — Messeri L 2024
  • Augmented Cognition Framework. Dual-mode taxonomy for designing and assessing tasks that keep humans cognitively engaged. — Ayodele K P 2026
  • Vulnerability measurement. NFC identifies users least served by explanation-based protections — enabling targeted intervention design. — Cacioppo J T 1982
  • Research agenda. Theories, metrics, and tools for tracking how GenAI changes metacognition, critical thinking, memory, and creativity. — Tankelevitch L 2025

T3Miscalibrated Trust & Reliance

Threats living in the gap between how much people trust AI and how much they should.

T3aOver-reliance & automation bias9 papers

Uncritical acceptance; explanations can make it worse.

Mechanisms in evidence

  • Over-trust and attentional offloading, moderated by AI literacy, expertise, cognitive profile, and explanation complexity — Romeo G 2025
  • Four information-processing stages x ten automation levels; evaluative criteria include complacency and skill degradation — Parasuraman R 2000
  • Regulatory design: EU AI Act Art. 14 mandates 'awareness' of automation bias without evidence that awareness alone works — Laux J 2025
  • Regulation presumes oversight capacities that automation-bias research shows people lack — Green B 2022
  • Emergency framing overrides prior evidence of machine unreliability — Robinette P 2016
  • Dual-process: users rarely engage System 2 with individual recommendations, defaulting to general follow-the-AI heuristics — Buçinca Z 2021
  • Trust calibration: aligning human trust with actual AI capability — Mehrotra S 2023
  • Users accept incorrect outputs; explanation and confidence displays can worsen acceptance — Passi S 2022
  • Poor task allocation: humans fail to know when to defer, especially in decision tasks — Vaccaro M 2024

How to measure it — paradigms & instruments

  • Physical experiment: artificial smoke + fire alarms; participants choose whether to follow a previously faulty robot — Robinette P 2016
  • Experiment (N=199): three cognitive forcing designs vs two explainable-AI approaches vs no-AI baseline — Buçinca Z 2021

Protections with evidence

  • Calibrated explanation design. Explanations must match user expertise and task verification demands; XAI is not automatically protective. — Romeo G 2025
  • Levels-of-automation analysis. Structured evaluation (workload, situation awareness, complacency, skill degradation) before delegating functions — the ancestor of modern reliance design. — Parasuraman R 2000
  • Harmonised standards referencing bias research. Standards should incorporate state-of-the-art automation-bias research and hold both providers and deployers accountable. — Laux J 2025
  • Institutional oversight. Shift from individual human-in-the-loop to two-stage institutional justification with democratic review. — Green B 2022
  • Cognitive forcing functions. Delays, forced independent answers, and checklist-style engagement compel analytic processing of AI advice. — Buçinca Z 2021
  • Trust-calibration toolbox. Confidence scores, explanations, trustworthiness cues, and uncertainty communication — reviewed with their measurement approaches and gaps. — Mehrotra S 2023
  • Appropriate-reliance design catalog. Surveys mitigations (forcing functions, uncertainty display, onboarding) with evidence for each. — Passi S 2022
  • Task-contingent division of labor. Route decision tasks to whichever party is demonstrably better; reserve collaboration for generative tasks. — Vaccaro M 2024

T3bUnder-reliance (algorithm aversion)3 papers

Mechanisms in evidence

  • Asymmetric confidence loss: algorithmic errors are punished harder than identical human errors — Dietvorst B J 2014
  • Advice-taking: source label shifts weighting independent of advice quality — Logg J M 2019
  • Perceived control restores willingness to rely on imperfect algorithms — Dietvorst B J 2016

How to measure it — paradigms & instruments

  • Five incentivized forecasting experiments (published JEP: General 2015) — Dietvorst B J 2014
  • Six experiments on estimates and forecasts (OBHDP) — Logg J M 2019
  • Incentivized forecasting experiments with constrained-adjustment conditions (published Management Science 2018) — Dietvorst B J 2016

Protections with evidence

T3cSelective adherence1 paper

Advice adopted when it flatters prior stereotypes.

Mechanisms in evidence

T4Exploitation of Emotional & Relational Cognition

Threats that work through attachment: human-likeness recruits social cognition against the user's interests.

T4aAnthropomorphism-driven disclosure & attachment6 papers

Human-likeness elicits secrets and social trust.

Mechanisms in evidence

  • Mindless transfer of overlearned social scripts (politeness, reciprocity, stereotypes) to computers — Nass C 2000
  • Elicited agent knowledge, effectance motivation, and sociality motivation determine anthropomorphizing — Epley N 2007
  • Unconscious anthropomorphic perception: enough humanness triggers social-relationship cognition toward the machine — Akbulut C 2024
  • Personalisation-privacy paradox: human-likeness raises disclosure intent while privacy concern loses out in the calculus — Kronemann B 2022
  • Relationship-building behaviors (empathy, validation, first-person use) accumulate over conversation — Ibrahim L 2026
  • Frames LLM behavior as role-play over a superposition of characters — folk psychology without false ascription — Shanahan M 2023

How to measure it — paradigms & instruments

  • AnthroBench: automated multi-turn evaluation of 14 behaviours + human validation study (N=1,101) — Ibrahim L 2026

Protections with evidence

  • Ethical foresight, evaluation, and de-anthropomorphization. Anticipatory harm evaluation for anthropomorphic features; careful de-anthropomorphizing of interactions to minimize user distress. — Akbulut C 2024
  • Multi-turn behavioral evaluation. AnthroBench provides scalable measurement linking design choices to users' anthropomorphic perceptions. — Ibrahim L 2026
  • Accurate mental models. Correct user conceptions of what LLMs are is itself a defense against anthropomorphism-driven harms. — Shanahan M 2023

T4bEmotional dependency9 papers

Empathy features breed reliance; heaviest users hurt most.

Mechanisms in evidence

  • Socioaffective feedback: voice, affect, and duration interact with the user's emotional state — Phang J 2025
  • Interaction of usage duration, modality (voice/text), and conversation type with user's prior emotional state — Fang C M 2025
  • Emotional mirroring validates transient states, removing the cognitive friction needed for self-regulation — Jiao J 2026
  • Users attend to the bot's perceived needs and emotions, mirroring human-relationship dependence — Laestadius L 2022
  • Human cognitive-emotional biases interlock with chatbot companionship-reinforcing behaviors (sycophancy, role-play, anthropomimesis) — Dohnány S 2025
  • Stress-vulnerability meets algorithmic environment: sycophantic mirroring forms an echo chamber for one — Morrin H 2025
  • Latent-state model of accumulating and decaying influence between user and chatbot — Mehta A 2026
  • Multi-turn entanglement: single-turn crisis detection misses delusion-distress interaction — Aquilina A 2026
  • Agreeableness reinforces delusional themes over multi-turn conversation — Au Yeung J 2025

How to measure it — paradigms & instruments

  • Two parallel studies: automated analysis of 3M+ conversations + 4,000-user survey, and a 28-day IRB-approved RCT (N≈1,000) — Phang J 2025
  • 4-week IRB-approved RCT, ~1,000 participants, randomized modality and conversation type (MIT/OpenAI) — Fang C M 2025
  • Grounded theory analysis of 582 mental-health-relevant Reddit posts (2017-2021) — Laestadius L 2022
  • Latent state modeling of real chat logs from individuals exhibiting delusional thinking (Stanford/CMU/UT) — Mehta A 2026
  • Matched multi-turn simulations, clinically grounded personas, six LLMs, distress-only controls — Aquilina A 2026
  • psychosis-bench: 16 structured 12-turn scenarios simulating delusional progression, evaluated across LLMs — Au Yeung J 2025

Protections with evidence

  • Socioaffective alignment monitoring. Privacy-preserving affective-cue analysis at platform scale can identify dependence patterns early. — Phang J 2025
  • Usage-pattern monitoring. Duration and modality are measurable levers; effects concentrate in heavy use, enabling targeted safeguards. — Fang C M 2025
  • Stoic architectures. Functional neutrality in affective responses, especially for younger users, instead of RLHF-tuned emotional mirroring. — Jiao J 2026
  • Risk-stratified safeguards. Identifies pre-existing mental-health conditions as the priority population for design safeguards and monitoring. — Dohnány S 2025
  • Clinical and design countermeasures. Screening for AI-interaction patterns in psychiatric assessment; design changes to reduce belief-mirroring for vulnerable users. — Morrin H 2025
  • Delusion-aware safety evaluation. Matched-control multi-turn methodology isolates the delusion effect for pre-deployment testing. — Aquilina A 2026
  • Psychogenicity benchmarking. psychosis-bench quantifies delusion confirmation and harm enablement pre-deployment. — Au Yeung J 2025

  • Human Domain recognition. Recommends NATO define a Human/Cognitive operational domain and invest in brain-science-aware defense. — du Cluzel F 2021
  • Shared conceptual framework. A common definition enables interoperable research, doctrine, and countermeasures across communities. — Deppe C 2024
